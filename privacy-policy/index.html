PRIVACY POLICY

Swarnakarshan Labs Private Limited

Last updated: 14th January 2026

Swarnakarshan Labs Private Limited ("SLPL", "we", "us", "our") operates digital platforms and services including KKOSH, designed to help users manage post-purchase ownership information and connect with service partners on a consent basis.

This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, share, and protect personal information when you use our website, mobile applications, or services ("Services").

1. Information We Collect

We collect only what is necessary to provide our Services.

a) Information you provide

  • Name, mobile number, email address
  • Ownership documents (invoices, warranties, insurance papers, service records)
  • Communication preferences and consent choices
  • Support requests or feedback

b) Information collected automatically

  • Device and browser information
  • IP address, timestamps, usage logs
  • Cookies and similar technologies (see Section 9)

c) Information from partners (with consent)

  • Service updates
  • Transaction or resolution status related to your request

2. Purpose of Collection

We use your information to:

  • Organise and manage ownership records
  • Send reminders for renewals, due dates, or expiries
  • Enable consent-based connection with verified partners or brands
  • Provide troubleshooting guidance and escalation support
  • Improve product performance, security, and reliability
  • Comply with legal and regulatory requirements

We do not sell personal data.

3. Consent-Based Partner Sharing

When you explicitly request help:

  • Your information is shared with only one relevant partner or brand
  • Sharing is purpose-limited and time-bound
  • Partners are contractually restricted from misuse

No broadcast, resale, or unsolicited outreach is permitted.

4. Legal Basis for Processing

We process personal data based on:

  • Your explicit consent
  • Performance of requested services
  • Compliance with applicable Indian laws (including the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023)

5. Data Retention

  • Data is retained only as long as required for the stated purpose
  • You may request deletion of your data at any time, subject to legal obligations
  • Aggregated or anonymised data may be retained for analytics

6. Data Security

We implement reasonable administrative, technical, and physical safeguards, including:

  • Access controls
  • Encryption where appropriate
  • Secure storage practices

No system is completely secure; however, we continuously improve safeguards.

7. Your Rights

Subject to applicable law, you may:

  • Access your personal data
  • Correct inaccurate information
  • Withdraw consent
  • Request data deletion
  • Raise grievances

We do not knowingly collect children's personal data.
